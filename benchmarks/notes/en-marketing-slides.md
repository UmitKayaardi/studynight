# Marketing Basics — Lecture 2

## Slide 1: What is marketing?
- Marketing = understanding what customers need and delivering value to them
- Not only advertising: includes research, product design, pricing and distribution
- Goal: build long-term relationships with customers

## Slide 2: The marketing mix (the 4 Ps)
- Product: what is sold, including its features, quality and design
- Price: what customers pay; affects how the product is seen
- Place: where and how customers can buy it (shops, online, distributors)
- Promotion: how customers learn about it (advertising, social media, sales offers)

## Slide 3: Market segmentation
- Divide a market into groups of customers with similar needs
- Common bases: demographic (age, income), geographic (region), behavioural (how often they buy)
- Targeting = choosing which segments to serve

## Slide 4: Positioning
- How a brand wants to be seen compared with competitors
- Example: a "premium" position supports higher prices
- A positioning statement names the target customer, the need and the key benefit

## Slide 5: Pricing strategies
- Penetration pricing: start with a low price to win market share quickly
- Price skimming: start high for early adopters, lower the price later
- Cost-plus pricing: add a fixed margin on top of the cost of making the product

## Slide 6: The product life cycle
- Introduction: sales are low, promotion costs are high
- Growth: sales rise quickly, competitors appear
- Maturity: sales level off, competition is strongest
- Decline: sales fall; the firm may update or withdraw the product

## Slide 7: Key takeaway
- Successful marketing starts with the customer, not the product
