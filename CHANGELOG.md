# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11

First release.

- Turn PDFs, PowerPoint, Word, text, Markdown, photos (including handwriting, best effort) or pasted text into a quiz
  in about half a minute; every question cites the exact passage it came from.
- Seven question types — multiple choice, true/false, fill in the blank, flashcards, match pairs, put in order,
  explain in your own words (typed or spoken) — plus figure questions and support for code and maths.
- Game modes: quick play, topic practice, review due, boss battle, exam mode with an estimated score band, sudden
  death, flashcards, challenge a friend (link only, no server).
- Ask your notes: answers only from your notes, with numbered citations, or says the notes don't cover it.
- Spaced repetition (FSRS), topic mastery, adaptive difficulty, XP and levels, streaks with freezes, 22 badges,
  session titles, exam planner with calendar export.
- Smart search: multilingual embeddings running on the device, with keyword search as the fallback.
- 21 interface languages including right-to-left Arabic, Persian and Urdu; quizzes in any language.
- Installable, works offline for anything already generated; six built-in sample decks work fully offline.
- Private by design: no accounts, analytics or cookies; all data stays on the device, with backup, import and delete.
